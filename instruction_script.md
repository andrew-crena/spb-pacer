# Final voice script — 4:6 paced breathing

## Delivery setting

- Voice quality: soft, even, conversational, not whispery and not a stylized meditation voice.
- Pace: about 1.8–2.0 words/second.
- Mic distance: 2–4 in / 5–10 cm, slightly off-axis.
- Leave short pauses after each sentence.
- Do not smile excessively into the delivery; keep it warm-neutral and instructional.
- No spoken reminders during the 10-minute pacing period unless piloting shows that participants lose the rhythm.

## Script

**0:00–0:10**  
In the next ten minutes, you'll breathe along with this circle.

**0:10–0:22**  
As it grows, breathe in gently through your nose. As it shrinks, breathe out slowly.

**0:22–0:34**  
Keep each breath light and comfortable. There's no need to breathe deeply.

**0:34–0:44**  
Sit comfortably and keep your eyes on the circle.

**0:44–0:56**  
If you feel dizzy or short of breath, breathe normally for a moment, then rejoin when you are ready.

**0:56–1:00**  
Let's practice three breaths together.

**First practice breath only**  
Breathe in.  
Breathe out.

**10-minute paced period**  
No narration.

**End**  
You can return to your normal breathing. The breathing exercise is complete. Please continue.

## Audio recommendation

Primary study version: voice at the start, silence during pacing, and one soft end chime. If ambience is tested during development, treat waves, rain, pink/brown noise, and phase tones as separate stimulus variants. Do not let participants choose among them during the experiment.
