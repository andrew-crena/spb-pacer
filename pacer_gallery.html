Instruction voice

In the next ten minutes, you'll breathe along with this circle.

As it grows, breathe in gently through your nose. As it shrinks, breathe out slowly.

Keep each breath light and comfortable. There's no need to breathe deeply.

Sit comfortably and keep your eyes on the circle.

If you feel dizzy or short of breath, breathe normally for a moment, then rejoin when you are ready.

Let's practice three breaths together.

Voice direction

Target: soft, close, conversational, and clear. Not whispered; not performatively meditative.

Pyle dynamic mic: 2–4 in / 5–10 cm away, 20–30° off-axis, pop filter or foam windscreen, mono WAV at 48 kHz. Aim for peaks around −12 to −6 dBFS.

Minimal processing: high-pass 70–90 Hz → light EQ if needed → 2:1 compression with roughly 2–4 dB gain reduction → mild de-essing if needed → normalize around −16 LUFS, peak below −1 dBTP.
