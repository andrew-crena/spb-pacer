In the next 10 minutes, you'll breathe along with a circle on the screen.

As it grows, breathe in gently through your nose. As it shrinks, breathe out slowly. Keep each breath light and comfortable. There's no need to breathe deeply.

Sit upright and keep your eyes on the circle. If you feel dizzy or short of breath, breathe normally for a moment, then rejoin the circle.

You'll start with three practice breaths. Please stay on this page until it finishes.
